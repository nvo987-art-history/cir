CONTENT DESCRIPTION:

CIR is a theoretical framework examining the structural
relationship between autonomous systems complexity and
human interpretive capacity.

CORE SUBJECTS:

- Autonomous systems
- Structural complexity
- Human interpretive capacity
- Technological transparency
- Delegated decision-making
- Responsibility structures
- Institutional interpretation
- Civilizational-scale systems

THEORETICAL POSITION:

CIR is a non-normative conceptual framework.

It does not promote or oppose technological development.

It examines the proportional relationship between structural
complexity and interpretive capacity.
